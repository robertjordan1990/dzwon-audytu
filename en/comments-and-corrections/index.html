Comments and corrections

The archive welcomes substantive comments, corrections and reports of errors relating to published materials.

What you can submit

  • A factual error.
  • A request for additional context.
  • A comment from a person or institution discussed in a publication.
  • A problem with a document, link, PDF, ZIP file or checksum.

How to submit

Email: robert.jordan.1990@proton.me.

Versioning policy

When making a correction, the archive may publish an addendum, erratum, comment or new version, while preserving a record of the earlier version and its status.

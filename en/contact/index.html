Contact and submitting materials

If you would like to provide documents, information or materials about the matters covered by Audit Bell, please contact:

robert.jordan.1990@proton.me

Where possible, please send documents in their original form, without converting them, renaming files or removing metadata. Particularly relevant materials include official correspondence, proof of service, UPO/UPP acknowledgements, ePUAP/e-Doręczenia confirmations, file metadata, responses to public-information requests and documents that help reconstruct how a matter was handled.

If the material contains sensitive personal information about third parties, please say so in your message. Any publication will be limited to what is necessary to demonstrate the systemic issue.

Supporting the audit

Audit Bell is run on a voluntary basis. Document analysis, archiving, maintaining the website and repository, internet access, electricity, backups and preparing publications involve real costs.

For comparison, a response from the Ministry of Science and Higher Education dated 10 June 2026 states that Fundacja Forum Akademickie received a total of PLN 3,648,000 in project funding during 2020–2026 under the “Social Responsibility of Science” and “Social Responsibility of Science II” programmes. The same response states that Fundacja Science Watch Polska received no funding from the Ministry during the period covered by the request.

If you would like to support the continued audit work, you can make a voluntary contribution towards the project’s technical and organisational costs.
